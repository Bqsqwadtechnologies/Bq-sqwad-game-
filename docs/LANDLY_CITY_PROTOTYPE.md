# Landly City Prototype

## Current Alpha Layout

The first playable district is intentionally small and testable. It is a foundation, not the final city art.

### Core spaces

- Central east-west road
- Central north-south road
- Four sidewalk corridors
- Repeating street-light network
- Six placeholder city buildings with procedural window blocks
- BQ Sqwad HQ and entrance plaza
- Training zone
- Mission zone
- Safe spawn area

## Build Philosophy

The prototype uses original procedural geometry so gameplay can be tested before final art is committed. Uploaded BQ Sqwad reference assets remain source material and are not silently treated as final game assets.

The city should be reviewed in this order:

1. Player scale and street scale
2. Road width and navigation flow
3. Building proportions and sight lines
4. HQ readability and landmark placement
5. Mission-zone accessibility
6. Mobile performance
7. Replacement of placeholders with original reviewed assets

## Next World Pass

The next pass should add modular building pieces, doors, rooftop silhouettes, road markings, traffic props, benches, barriers, signs, vegetation, interior shells, and stronger district identity. Final models and textures should be derived from the project's own references and original BQ Sqwad visual direction rather than copied from existing games or real-world copyrighted designs.
