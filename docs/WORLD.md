# Landly City — World Foundation

## First district

The initial district is intentionally small and playable. It should establish the visual language and gameplay scale of Landly City without requiring the complete city to exist.

### Required locations

- BQ Sqwad Headquarters
- Main city street
- Residential block
- Commercial block
- Mission zone
- Training area
- Safe player spawn

### Expansion model

Landly City will be divided into districts so new areas can be added without replacing the existing world. Each district should define its landmarks, gameplay purpose, NPC population, missions, traversal routes, and streaming boundaries.

### Art direction

Premium cinematic science-fiction/superhero atmosphere, with a distinctive BQ Sqwad identity. Avoid copying protected characters, logos, maps, or visual designs from other games.
